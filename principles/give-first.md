# Give first (reciprocity)

**Memory hook:** a gift creates a debt.

## The idea
When someone gives us something first, we feel a pull to return the favor.
It isn't rational; it's one of the deepest human instincts.
Most apps do the opposite: "sign up to see your results", "create an
account to continue". The user hasn't received anything and the app
already wants something. Deliver real value first, then ask.

## Why it works
Persuasion researcher Robert Cialdini ranks reciprocity among the most
powerful drivers of behavior. Receiving something creates an unconscious
debt, so a request that comes after value feels like an invitation to
continue, not a wall. Asking first feels like a restaurant asking for
your card before showing you the menu: people walk out.

## Before / after
- **Before:** the user enters their website, taps "Analyze", waits for the
  scan, and the results appear blurred behind "Create an account to see
  your report". The results are held hostage.
- **After:** the user gets a real report right away: their score, top
  issues, what passed. Enough to be genuinely useful. At the bottom:
  "Want the complete breakdown with step-by-step fixes? Save your report."
- **Real-world examples:** free samples in stores, Spotify's free premium
  trial, Notion letting you use the full product before paying.

## Where it applies
- **Sign-up:** let people try the core feature before creating an account.
- **Permissions:** show what the feature does before asking for camera,
  location or notifications.
- **Processing results:** after scanning, analyzing or importing, show the
  useful result first, then offer to save or organize it.
- **Sharing:** show a preview of what will be shared before asking for a contact.
- **Paid features:** give a meaningful free part, then offer the full version.

## Do
- Make the first result genuinely useful on its own.
- Ask at the moment the user wants more ("Save this", "See the full breakdown").

## Don't
- Blur or lock a result the user just waited for.
- Ask for an account, permission or payment before showing anything.

## Guardrail
The free part must be real value, not a teaser that is useless on its own.
